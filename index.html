AUTOBIOGRAPHY

I am Ivan Joas B. Managat, a Computer Science student at the University of the Philippines Los Baños with a deep passion for web development and digital design. My interest in this field began when I started college and had the opportunity to explore different areas of computing. It was fascinating to see how coding and creativity could come together to build meaningful digital experiences.

I decided to pursue web development because every time I encountered a website with exceptional design and seamless user experience, I felt inspired. I realized that I wanted to create something that could make people feel the same way. For me, web creation is not just about writing code. It is a form of art where logic meets creativity and structure blends with imagination.

Beyond development, I am also passionate about graphic design. I enjoy transforming ideas into visual concepts and creating designs that are both engaging and purposeful. I believe that a great digital experience should not only function well but also connect with people visually and emotionally.

As I continue growing in this field, I aim to refine both my technical skills and creative vision. I want to build projects that showcase innovation, usability, and artistic expression, proving that technology can be both powerful and beautiful.
